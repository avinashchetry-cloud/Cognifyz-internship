# Restaurant Geographical Distribution and Rating Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores the geographical distribution of restaurants and analyzes variations in restaurant ratings across different cities.

The objective is to understand restaurant concentration across geographical regions and investigate whether restaurant ratings vary depending on location.

## Objectives

- Visualize restaurant locations using geographical coordinates.
- Identify regions with high restaurant concentration.
- Analyze city-wise average restaurant ratings.
- Identify the top 10 cities based on average restaurant ratings.
- Explore the relationship between geographical location and restaurant ratings.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Folium
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Geographical Distribution

- Used Latitude and Longitude to identify restaurant locations.
- Created an interactive geographical map using Folium.
- Implemented MarkerCluster to group nearby restaurant markers.
- Observed restaurant concentration across different geographical regions.

### 2. City-wise Rating Analysis

- Grouped restaurants by city.
- Calculated average restaurant ratings.
- Identified the top 10 cities based on average ratings.
- Visualized the results using a horizontal bar chart.

## Key Findings

- India showed a significantly higher concentration of restaurants in the dataset.
- Restaurant clusters were also observed in regions of North America and Africa.
- Inner City recorded the highest average restaurant rating among the top 10 cities.
- The average ratings of the top 10 cities were relatively close, indicating only small differences among them.

## Conclusion

The analysis provided insights into the geographical distribution of restaurants and variations in average ratings across cities.

Interactive mapping helped identify restaurant clusters, while city-wise rating analysis highlighted differences in restaurant performance across locations.

However, differences in average ratings alone do not establish that geographical location directly influences restaurant ratings.
